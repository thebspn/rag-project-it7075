# Turn Structure and Player Actions

Each player's turn in the Pokemon TCG proceeds systematically through three sequential phases: the Draw Step, the Main Phase, and the Attack Step. Understanding the strict boundaries between these phases is fundamental for proper play sequencing.

## The Draw Step
The active player begins their turn by drawing one card from the top of their deck. Drawing a card during this step is mandatory; failing to draw because the deck has zero remaining cards results in an immediate loss by deckout. The player going first in a tournament match is bound by initial turn restrictions: they cannot play a Supporter card from hand on their first turn and cannot declare an attack on turn one.

## The Main Phase
During the Main Phase, the active player may perform an array of tactical actions in any order before choosing to declare an attack:
- Play any number of Basic Pokemon from hand onto the Bench, up to a maximum bench capacity of five Pokemon.
- Evolve active or benched Pokemon, provided the pre-evolution card has been in play for at least one full turn.
- Attach one Energy card from hand to any Pokemon currently in play (limited to exactly once per turn).
- Retreat the Active Pokemon to the Bench by paying its printed Retreat Cost using attached Energy (limited to once per turn).
- Play any number of Item cards and Pokemon Tool cards within legal hand limits.
- Play exactly one Supporter card per turn, resolving its instructions and placing it in the discard pile.
- Play exactly one Stadium card per turn, discarding any preexisting Stadium card in play.

## The Attack Step
The Attack Step represents the definitive conclusion of the active turn. To declare an attack, the Active Pokemon must possess the required energy types and quantities attached to its card. Once damage calculations, weakness multipliers, resistance reductions, and primary attack text effects resolve, the turn concludes immediately. If the active player cannot attack or prefers not to attack, they announce that they pass the turn to the opponent.

# Special Conditions and Status Ailments

Special Conditions are status ailments that afflict only the active defending or attacking Pokemon. Benched Pokemon cannot suffer from Special Conditions, and moving an afflicted Pokemon to the Bench immediately removes and cures all active status effects. There are five distinct Special Conditions: Asleep, Burned, Confused, Paralyzed, and Poisoned.

## Orientation-Based Conditions
Three conditions are physically indicated by rotating the card sleeve:
- Asleep: The card is turned sideways ninety degrees counter-clockwise. An Asleep Pokemon cannot attack or retreat manually. Between turns during the Pokemon Checkup step, the player flips a coin; heads wakes the Pokemon up and returns it upright, while tails leaves it asleep.
- Paralyzed: The card is turned sideways ninety degrees clockwise. A Paralyzed Pokemon cannot attack or execute a retreat action. Unlike sleep, Paralysis cures automatically at the conclusion of the affected player's following turn without requiring a coin flip.
- Confused: The card is turned completely upside down (180 degrees). When a Confused Pokemon attempts to attack, its owner must flip a coin. If heads, the attack succeeds normally. If tails, the attack fails completely, and the attacking Pokemon suffers thirty damage to itself.

## Marker-Based Conditions
Poisoned and Burned utilize dedicated physical counters rather than card orientation:
- Poisoned: Indicated with a Poison marker. During every Pokemon Checkup phase between turns, exactly one damage counter (representing ten damage) is placed onto the Poisoned Pokemon.
- Burned: Indicated with a Burn marker. During Pokemon Checkup, two damage counters (twenty damage) are placed onto the Burned Pokemon, followed by a mandatory coin flip. Heads removes the Burn marker, while tails keeps the condition active.

A Pokemon can be Poisoned and Burned simultaneously, but orientation-based conditions overwrite one another; if an Asleep Pokemon becomes Paralyzed, Paralysis replaces the sleep status immediately.
